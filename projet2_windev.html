Review Platform

More détail

The platform is a tool that serves both to allow clients to give feedback about a trainer after a training session and to enable managers to assess the performance of these trainers in order to improve it.

My task was to transition from paper to a platform on the website of GMA Consulting.

Initially, I started by working on the specifications with my supervisor, followed by the database structure and queries.

Next, I began creating a visual mockup in Webdev that reflects the main ideas of the specifications.

The challenge was to create a platform using Windev and Webdev.
