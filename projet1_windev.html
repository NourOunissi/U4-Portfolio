Debugging code-level issues

More details

The GMA Consulting website is the one I worked on during my first year. It is an application with a rights management system, where clients can access their license(s), and technical support has permissions to view and modify client accounts.

My task was initially to retrieve the bug report file and organize the issues by type. In the second phase, I worked on fixing these problems locally and tested them again. Finally, I documented the solutions found, or if the issue wasn't resolved, I recorded the procedures and paths attempted.

This project uses the organization's data.
