About me

Curious and interested in new technologies in the field of computer science from a young age, I have always been drawn to their advancements. After studying for two years at a medical university, I decided to join the army as a front-line fighter in the artillery. After three years of service, I chose to transition to a new career and return to my studies in computer science in order to bring my project to life.

I began learning the basics of computer science on my own at home using platforms like OpenClassrooms. Later on, I wanted to solidify my knowledge and turn it into skills.

Before enrolling for my associate degree, I worked independently for one year as a self-taught web developer. I obtained my degree as a full stack developer while simultaneously gaining one year of experience at GMA Consulting, a French company specializing in software development for local authorities, as well as training, consulting, and support since 2004. After this first experience, I worked for one year at Kurz Informatique, a French company specializing in web application and software development since 2016. I am currently seeking new professional opportunities, either as a freelancer or within a company, to continue progressing and further developing my expertise.
